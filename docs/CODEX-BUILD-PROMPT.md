## Non-negotiable product and business rules

1. **Employer/partner-funded model — latest owner decision.** Candidates are not charged placement, application/referral, success or salary-deduction fees. No candidate-side charge/term may depend on employment. Revenue comes from employers and partners only. This supersedes historical candidate-paid price lists/proposals, including the R100 Job Application Service and previous CV-service prices; do not reactivate them or invent an exemption. It is Neo's chosen business policy, distinct from a blanket legal conclusion about every career service. Preserve historical records with superseded labels. Actual billing, fee formulae, contracts or guarantees need separate approval and legal review; do not change them silently.
2. **Evidence, not invented merit.** Never invent qualifications, experience, certifications, references, placements, salaries, contacts or hiring volume. Label candidate-declared, document-supported and independently checked information separately. Verification records need scope, source reference, reviewer, date and result; gaps remain explicit. A stored Verified flag is not evidence by itself.
3. **Explainable matching.** Preserve MATCHED / MISSING / NOT EVALUATED. Percentage is rounded matched / evaluable requirements × 100, null when none are evaluable; legacy unevaluable requirements remain visible outside the denominator. Matching is informational, not a hiring/proficiency/ATS score. Search remains independent; matching never advances the application lifecycle or decides eligibility. Humans review consequential employment decisions; no discriminatory proxies or employment guarantees.
4. **Privacy and consent.** Give the appropriate notice and record purpose-specific consent/choice before requested candidate intake; do not treat existing account, group membership or one checkbox as permission for all processing. Map minimal identity/security processing to its lawful purpose/basis rather than claiming it begins only after intake consent. Optional talent-pool and marketing choices are separate and off by default. Follow the approved effective schedule when one exists; docs/RETENTION-POLICY-DRAFT.md remains a draft until adoption and implementation. Privacy PR #9 is merged at this snapshot; v0.2 is still a draft, not adopted policy or a working erasure process. Minimise/redact unnecessary ID numbers, photos, health/race and other restricted information. ID numbers/photos are not automatically special personal information; apply the actual POPIA classification. No purge, deletion/de-identification or live-record experiment without Neo's written approval, scope and reviewed plan. Preserve lawful holds/register requirements.
5. **Security.** Fail closed. Verify Clerk signature/algorithm, issuer/JWKS, expiry/session claims and exact authorised origin; resolve roles from persisted assignments. Current sessions have no configured custom aud: do not claim audience validation exists, and do not add a mandatory audience blindly that breaks accepted sign-in. If an audience is introduced, approve/configure/test the complete contract. Candidate ownership, company scope and vacancy-level recruiter assignment are server-side requirements. No public role self-promotion, fake production principals, credential extraction, secret commits or weakened deployment protection.
6. **Authority and external effects.** Feature branches and reviewable commits/PRs only; never edit main directly. No PR merge, main promotion, production release/environment change or live migration without applicable explicit approval. Inspect the exact approval's scope; approval of an earlier PR is not approval of every future PR. Airtable, Figma and Vercel are read-only by default under this mandate. No purchase, new provider, paid resource, real candidate-record access/change, destructive/rewriting schema change or fee-logic change without the required approval. Prepare concrete reviewable work first. Read-only preview checks remain allowed; mutations/tests use synthetic disposable environments. Never contact partners/team/candidates, send outreach or file/sign registrations unless explicitly instructed for that action.
