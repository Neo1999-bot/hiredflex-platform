## Data and access

Employer routes use existing `GET /employer/vacancies`, `GET /employer/companies` and `GET /employer/vacancies/:id/applications`. Selection is restricted to the loaded authorised vacancy list; backend company isolation remains authoritative. Pipeline is read-only; assigned Recruiter actions remain in the existing recruiter workspace. No new database schema, migration, role provisioning or access escalation is introduced. Opening employer/support pages does not approve a business or send outreach.
