# HiredFlex Pty Ltd preparation — owner review draft

Prepared 25 October 2026. Nothing has been filed, paid, signed or registered by this work. Keep completed private founder/director/shareholder information outside public GitHub. These are preparation prompts and unsigned templates, not completed statutory forms or legal advice for an executed transaction.

## Owner checklist

- [ ] Choose the registration route and verify its current requirements and checkout price. CIPC lists standard and customised MOI routes. Its standard private-company guidance requires at least one director and incorporator (which may be the same person), and an MOI. A customised MOI warrants appropriate professional review.
- [ ] Choose a name reservation route or registration without a reserved name where the selected service permits it. Domain availability does not establish company-name approval or trade-mark clearance.
- [ ] Confirm proposed company name alternatives, business purpose, registered-office/contact details, financial year end and lawful director eligibility. Do not infer these from a prototype or a social profile.
- [ ] Confirm each incorporator/director's legal details and consent, shareholder interests, share classes/control and beneficial ownership privately. Prepare identification, address and mandate evidence securely if required by the selected route; do not assume the same upload requirements across BizPortal and other services.
- [ ] Review the MOI and any shareholders'/founders' agreement, IP ownership, decision rights, reserved matters, vesting/leaver terms and conflicts with an appropriate professional before signing.
- [ ] Neo completes authentication, reviews all statutory declarations and pays/files only after approving the exact transaction. Retain the actual certificate/MOI and source receipts privately after completion.
- [ ] Check CIPC beneficial-ownership filing, authorised-filer mandate, supporting-register requirements and current deadlines directly before filing; do not rely on a guessed deadline. Establish annual-return and ownership-change reminders from actual registration evidence.
- [ ] Confirm the company Income Tax reference and authorised SARS/eFiling representative after registration. SARS states CIPC registrations interface to generate an Income Tax registration; this does not mean all tax types are registered. Assess VAT/PAYE/UIF/SDL and other obligations with the business's actual activities/staff and applicable thresholds.
- [ ] Prepare business banking using the selected bank's current requirements. Incorporation alone is not a bank-account opening.
- [ ] Confirm employment-agency obligations applicable to the service model and candidate charging/referral arrangements before offering recruitment services under unreviewed terms.
- [ ] Complete the applicable POPIA/PAIA governance: information officer registration, notices, purpose/legal basis, access/correction requests, retention/legal holds, security and operator/provider agreements. A consent tick alone is not a complete compliance programme.
- [ ] Treat WhatsApp Business/API verification as a separate provider process involving business evidence, number ownership and an approved provider/cost decision. Registration is not automatic API approval.

## Equity and vesting discussion — no allocation agreed

- Contributions: cash, work, IP and evidence; distinguish previous effort from future commitments.
- Cap table scenarios: total shares, classes, percentage/control, dilution and financing; no invented current ownership.
- Vesting options to discuss: duration, cliff, commencement, milestones, leaver treatment, repurchase and tax consequences. Do not treat a common schedule as mandatory law.
- Governance: board/management roles, reserved matters, deadlock, conflicts and information rights.
- IP assignment/licensing and existing third-party/workplace obligations.
- Transfer/pre-emption, exit, dispute and succession arrangements; review with a suitably qualified professional before signing.
